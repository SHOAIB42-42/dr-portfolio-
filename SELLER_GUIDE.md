# 🩺 MedFolio Pro — Doctor Portfolio & Clinical Resume System

**MedFolio Pro** is a modern, high-converting Doctor Portfolio and Academic Resume web template designed for medical professionals, fresh MBBS/FCPS graduates, and hospital consultants.

---

## 💼 How to Sell This Template to Doctors for Profit

1. **Target Audience**:
   - Fresh MBBS/FCPS graduates applying for Senior Registrar or Consultant jobs.
   - Private Clinic Doctors looking to increase OPD patient appointments.
   - Medical Professors needing a research publication portfolio.

2. **Fast 5-Minute Client Customization**:
   - All doctor details are centralized in `src/data/doctorData.js`.
   - Open `src/data/doctorData.js` and update:
     - Doctor Name & Medical Registration # (e.g. PMC / PMDC / GMC)
     - Degrees (MBBS, FCPS, MRCP, etc.) & Institutions
     - Clinical Work History & Hospitals
     - Practice Locations & OPD Timings
     - Direct WhatsApp Phone Number
     - Doctor Avatar Image URL
   - Save the file and your client's custom portfolio is 100% ready!

3. **Free Hosting Options for Clients**:
   - Host for free on **Vercel**, **Netlify**, or **GitHub Pages**.
   - Connect custom domains (e.g., `www.drayesharahman.com`).

---

## 🚀 Commands

- **Run Locally (Development)**: `npm run dev`
- **Build Production Files**: `npm run build` (Outputs ready-to-deploy files in `/dist`)
- **Preview Production Build**: `npm run preview`

## ⭐ Key Features Included

- 👨‍⚕️ **Verified Medical Badge**: Official Council Reg # verification card.
- 🎓 **Academic & Clinical Timeline**: Degrees, certifications, and hospital experience.
- 🩺 **Specialization & OPD Services**: Service cards with instant appointment triggers.
- 📄 **Research & PubMed Section**: Indexed journal publications with DOI links.
- 🏥 **Multi-Hospital Practice Schedule**: Hospital tabs with OPD fees & Google Maps directions.
- 📅 **Direct WhatsApp Appointment Launcher**: Converts patient inquiries into structured WhatsApp messages.
- 📄 **1-Click Printable Medical CV**: Printable Curriculum Vitae modal formatted for job applications.
- 🌙 **Dark/Light Mode**: Smooth medical theme toggle.
